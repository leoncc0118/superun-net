經營團隊

Cosmic Mining is marketing Legal Steam Coal, mined from our Partners (KP Holders) with the Open-Pit-Method, which is a cost effective, safe and efficient method of coal removal. The Overburden is low and the Pit Area is wide, that allows simultaneously work of different types of equipment.

經營團隊包括實力堅強，擁有高科技產業專長，另有具備多年產業經驗。深厚的產業基礎及人脈結合而成的宏觀視野團隊的特色，掌握未來產業的趨勢，而對各方面的廣泛的團隊具備企業管理組織的能力，能提供被投資公司更多的加值服務，強化其競爭優勢。

設備儀器

Coal Processing Plant

Coal delivered from the mine site to the main stockpile in raw coal then processed and crushed to meet the requirement. The crushing plant capacity is 3,500 – 4,000 MT per day. Quality control becomes the highest priority in the section to obtain consistent product quality. Coal product will be piled separately.

Stockpile & Jetty

PT.Cosmic Mining Services has signed a contract to operate a Jetty in Asem-Asem of 20 Hectares area. A new conveyor system capable of loading 1,200MT/Hour is to be installed in 5 months.
